Before you enable SORT

Recording disclosure · version 1.0

SORT stays off until you agree. GoodBytz GmbH uses SORT for designated customer-support and troubleshooting sessions. Please read what is recorded and how it is used.

What is recorded

  • Screen video, without audio: the screen, window or tab you select in Chrome’s sharing dialog.
  • Structured activity across browser tabs: clicks, scrolling, keyboard interactions, entered field values, page titles, URLs, navigation, network-request information and WebSocket message payloads. This is not limited to the source selected for video and can include data not visible on screen.
  • Recordings can identify you and customers, including your configured Sipgate name, caller names and linkable system or ticket identifiers. Password-field masking is not comprehensive redaction. Avoid unrelated personal or sensitive content.

How GoodBytz uses it

  • Documenting and resolving support cases and debugging and improving products.
  • Reviewing support quality, individual coaching and improving team processes. The stated purposes do not include automated employee scoring or pay, promotion or disciplinary decisions.
  • AI training, fine-tuning and evaluation: only human-reviewed, de-identified structured-timeline extracts on company-controlled systems. No video is used for AI. De-identification does not necessarily mean anonymity.

Access, storage and transmission

Recordings are stored locally and can be uploaded to GoodBytz’s recording server. Access is limited by role to authorized recording employees, support/service staff, engineering/AI staff and team leads/management. AI use is limited to the reviewed extracts. Odoo stores a recording link, not a copy of the video or timeline.

The upload server, Odoo and company-controlled AI systems are hosted in Germany. Uploads use HTTP through the mandatory company VPN, not HTTPS at the upload endpoint. SORT does not independently enforce VPN connectivity or guarantee encryption beyond VPN termination.

Retention

Uploaded recordings and AI extracts are deleted one month after the original recording date; uploaded recordings have no server backups. Odoo recording links are removed with the recording. You must manually delete local recordings and exports within the same period. Deleting AI extracts does not automatically remove their influence from trained models; underlying tickets have separate retention.

Other local data and services

SORT uses your configured name, Odoo email/API key and call-state token. Credentials remain in this Chrome profile without additional application-level encryption, not Chrome Sync. The relevant services receive requests and credentials needed for their functions. Automatic call-state polling runs only while SORT is on and configured.

After agreement, bounded diagnostic logs are retained locally for up to 7 days, 5 MiB or 5,000 entries, whichever limit is reached first. Diagnostic exports are user-requested and are not automatically uploaded.

Your controls and this agreement

Enabling SORT does not itself begin a screen recording. Recordings can be started manually or triggered by relevant calls while SORT is on. Chrome still asks you to choose a video source. Cancelling that choice cancels both video and timeline recording. Switching SORT off stops and saves an active recording and blocks new manual and automatic recordings.

Your agreement is saved in this Chrome profile with this disclosure’s version and a timestamp. It is not requested again on normal off/on switches or browser restarts. A materially revised disclosure or cleared extension data requires agreement again. The local record is not an employee signature or a centralized acknowledgment record.

Recording remains required for designated work activities under company policy. This agreement does not by itself establish freely given GDPR consent for mandatory workplace processing. Questions or rights requests: leon.weber@goodbytz.com.
